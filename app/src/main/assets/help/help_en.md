# {icon:Help} Complete Vixa guide

Welcome to the guide for **Vixa by DSK**, a full video editor that fits in your pocket. Everything works **offline** (except online resources and automatic music) and your videos never leave your phone.

> **How to use this guide:** the **index** and the **search box** are always at the top. Tap any title in the index to jump to it, or type a word (for example *keyframes*, *subtitles* or *GIF*) to see every place it appears. The icons you see here are **the same ones the app uses** in its tools.

If this is your first time, start with the **Video assistant** and then **Getting started**. If you already know how to edit, use the search box to go straight to the tool you need.

# {icon:AutoAwesome} 1. The Video assistant

The assistant is the fastest way to get a finished video: you pick your photos and videos, a **style** and the **music**, and it builds the cuts **on the beat of the song**, with transitions, camera moves, color, a title and a closing. You end up with a **regular project** that opens in the editor and that you can tweak any way you like.

You will find it on the **home screen**, in the colored card at the top: **Video assistant**.

## Your first video in 2 minutes

1. On the home screen tap the **Video assistant** card.
2. Tap **Choose media** and select several photos and videos (you can mix them).
3. *(Optional)* Type a **title** and a **closing message** (for example "Thanks for watching").
4. Pick a **style**. If in doubt, leave **Surprise me**.
5. Leave the music on **Automatic** (the assistant finds it) or pick your own.
6. Tap **Continue**. You will see the list of your media to **sort** by dragging the {icon:DragHandle} icon.
7. Tap **Create edit**. Wait a few seconds while it analyzes and builds.
8. Watch the **preview**. If you like it, tap **Open in the editor**.

> **Tip:** nothing is saved until you tap **Open in the editor**. You can try as many times as you want without filling your project list.

### What the assistant does with each style
- **Sections:** the video starts and ends with longer shots and soft transitions; in the **climax** (towards the end) it uses the most intense transitions and, in fast styles, cuts twice as fast.
- **Beat effects:** zoom hits, flashes or shakes right on the beats of the music. They are regular keyframes: you can adjust them in the editor.
- **Ambience layer:** an adjustment layer over the whole video (confetti, snow, bokeh, VHS, cinema bars…). If you do not want it, delete it on the timeline.
- **Videos:** calm styles play them in slow motion and energetic ones may add speed ramps (only when their original sound is not heard).

**Surprise me** picks one at random or, if you use your own song, the one that best fits its tempo.

## Music

You have three options:

- **Automatic:** the assistant searches **Jamendo** for **instrumental** music that fits the style (tags and speed), picks one and downloads it. Needs a connection. It is *Creative Commons* music: if you plan to publish the video, check its license.
- **My song:** you pick one of your own audio files. The assistant analyzes its **rhythm** on the phone.
- **No music:** cuts follow the style's own tempo.

### How it follows the beat
The assistant detects the beats of the song and fits a very precise **beat grid** (it computes tempo and phase so it does not drift over a minute of music). Cuts land on the beats and the **center of each transition lands exactly on the beat**. Every 8 beats (a "phrase") it uses a longer **accent** transition. If it cannot find a clear rhythm, it tells you and uses the style's tempo.

### Songs with a long intro
If the song takes a while to get going, the assistant **skips the intro**: it starts a couple of beats before the music kicks in, with a fade in, so the video does not start flat.

## Original sound and lowering the music

- **Keep the original sound of the videos:** by default, with music, videos are muted. Tick this box to keep their sound (the music gets quieter).
- **Lower the music when a voice is heard** *(on by default)*: detects speech in the videos —with the voice model if you have it downloaded, otherwise by volume— and lowers the music **only while it is heard**. You can turn it off.

## Color fade

Choose **None**, **At the start**, **At the end** or **Start and end**. With a fade on, a **color** picker appears (black by default). The fade is a color clip with animated opacity, so you can tweak it later in the editor.

## Social media presets

One tap fills in format and duration:

| Preset | Format | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok · Reels · Shorts | 9:16 | 30 s |
| Stories | 9:16 | 15 s |
| Instagram | 4:5 | 30 s |
| YouTube | 16:9 | Natural |

## Format, pace and duration

- **Format:** each aspect ratio shows a **drawing** of its shape. **Auto** picks landscape or portrait depending on your media.
- **Pace:** the slider goes from **Calm** (×0.5, shots twice as long) to **Fast** (×2).
- **Duration:** *Natural* uses all the media; there are also 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 and 180 s, and **Custom** (5 s to 1 hour). Shots get shorter or longer in whole beats. If the song is shorter, **it repeats**; if there is not enough media, it repeats too (with a different section of the videos).

## Smart options

- **Smart framing and zoom:** looks for **faces** and points of interest in the photos. When the format changes it crops toward them (if they fit) instead of leaving bars, and the slow zoom (*Ken Burns*) aims at the face.
- **Best section of long videos** *(on by default)*: instead of using the start of each video, scores each section by **sharpness**, some **motion** (neither static nor shaky), **no scene cuts** and, if heard, that there is **audio**. Turn it off if you prefer the edit to be generated faster.
- **Automatic subtitles:** transcribes the speech of the videos (needs the AI models downloaded) and adds it as subtitles.
- **Generate three proposals** *(off by default)*: builds three videos at once so you can choose. **Takes more time.**

## Order, preview and retry

- In **Order** you can **drag** the {icon:DragHandle} icon, **remove** media with {icon:Close}, sort **By date** (uses the date of the photo or video) or **Shuffle** {icon:Shuffle}.
- The **preview** first prepares a small video (so it plays **smoothly**, without cuts) and plays it with {icon:PlayArrow}/{icon:Pause} and a bar to move around. If you do not want to wait, tap **Watch live now**.
- Preview buttons: **Modify** (go back), **Another version** (everything again), **Another song**, **Other transitions** (same music and style) and **Open in the editor**.

## If something is not as expected

- **No music:** you may be offline. The edit is created anyway and you can add music later.
- **"No clear rhythm was detected":** the song is very irregular; cuts follow the style's tempo.
- **I want to change something afterwards:** open it in the editor; everything (clips, transitions, text) is editable.

Tap **New project** and choose:

# {icon:Dashboard} 3. The editor at a glance

The editor screen is organized like this (in portrait, top to bottom):

1. **Top bar:** back, project name, {icon:Undo} **Undo**, {icon:Redo} **Redo** and **Export**.
2. **Preview:** what you see in the video. Elements are selected and moved here.
3. **Playback controls:** time, previous/next frame, play/pause, loop, guides and fullscreen.
4. **Timeline:** the tracks with your clips.
5. **Toolbar:** with nothing selected it shows the project tools; with a clip selected, that clip's tools.

In **landscape** or on a tablet, the preview goes on one side, the tools on the other and the timeline below.

> **Tip:** long-press a toolbar button to see its **name**.

## Preview

- **Tapping** an element selects it; drag it to move it. With **two fingers** you scale and rotate it.
- With **nothing selected**, pinch with two fingers to **zoom the view** (only the view, not the video). The {icon:PanTool} **hand** (to drag the view) and {icon:FitScreen} **reset** appear.
- Magnetic **guides** help you center and align.
- The gray area around the canvas is also touchable, so you can reach elements that stick out.

## Fullscreen

With {icon:Fullscreen} the preview takes the whole screen **just for watching**: you cannot select or move anything by accident. Drag across the bar to scrub the whole edit and use the play and start/end buttons.

## Undo, redo and history

{icon:Undo} and {icon:Redo} undo and redo. The {icon:History} **History** tool lists the steps: tap one to **go back to it**. History is kept even if you close the app (last 25 steps).

## Timeline

- **Drag** to move along it; **pinch** with two fingers to zoom in or out.
- **Tap** a clip to select it and drag its end **handles** to **trim** it.
- **Long-press and drag** to move a clip or change its order.
- Track types: **main** (video and image, "magnetic": clips follow each other), **overlay**, **audio**, **text**, **stickers** and **adjustment**.
- The **+** button under the timeline adds media, audio, text or stickers; on the main track, the button between two clips is the **transition** button.
- Long-press an **empty track** to delete it (it asks for confirmation).
- **Magnetic snapping** snaps the playhead and clips to cuts and markers.

---

## Pro mode and Normal mode

At the top of the editor there is a **Pro** button. It comes **on** and shows every tool. If you turn it **off** you switch to **Normal mode**: only the main tools are shown and the animation diamonds (keyframes) on the timeline are hidden. The app **remembers your choice**: new projects open in the mode you left it in.

## Editor view settings

In **Settings** you can turn three things on or off (they come on by default):

- **Simple view:** with a panel open (Text, Stickers, Transform, Animate, Filters, Color adjustments…) the timeline is hidden and you see the video and the panel. Panels that need the playhead (keyframes, voice-over, subtitles, masks) keep the timeline.
- **Quick bar:** when you select a clip, a small bar over the video offers split, duplicate and delete.
- **Big cards:** fonts (shown with their "Aa"), filters and animations look bigger.

## Draw a path for any element

On any element that can be animated (text, sticker, photo, overlay video…) open **Animate** and tap **Draw a path**. You will see your video behind: draw with your finger where it should move and tap **Done ✓**. The position keyframes are created for you. To remove it tap **Remove path**.

If you later change the element's **duration** (for example, you make it longer):

- In **Pro mode** the app **asks** whether to adjust the movement to the new duration or leave it as is.
- In **Normal mode** it **adjusts it directly**, without asking.

## Split the screen between preview and timeline

**Hold for 1 second** the transport bar (the one with the clock and the play button, on a gap between buttons; look for the small handle at the top) and drag it **up or down**: the preview grows and the timeline shrinks, or the other way around. **Double tap** the bar and everything goes back to normal.

# {icon:Add} 4. Adding media

When you tap **Download and insert** on an **Online** tab, the item is **inserted right away** (together with anything you had already ticked), without going through the button at the top.

Tap {icon:Add} **Add media** (or the timeline **+** button). The picker opens with three tabs:

- **Device:** *Gallery*, *Audio*, *Files*, *Recent* and **Camera** (take a photo or video now).
- **Online:** search for free images, illustrations, videos, GIFs, music and sound effects in several sources at once (see *Online resources*).
- **Color:** solid or gradient color clips.

Select one or several and tap **Add**. On the main track they are added **after the last clip**.

## Color tab

- Type: **Solid**, **Linear** or **Radial**; black, white or custom.
- **Gradients** of 2 to 4 colors with presets. Tap a color to change it; its **opacity** control makes that side **transparent**. You can **invert** and set the **angle**.
- **Quick actions:** *Add black at start / at end*, *Fade from black*, *Fade to black*.
- Clip **duration** and the **Add color clip** button.

## Overlay, audio and more

- {icon:PictureInPicture} **Overlay:** adds a video or photo **on top of** the main one (picture in picture).
- {icon:MusicNote} **Audio:** when you pick a song longer than 2 s it asks whether you want it **whole** or a **section** with two sliders. It is not a destructive cut: later you can stretch the handles and the rest of the track appears.
- **Animated GIFs and WebPs** ask whether you want them **animated** or as a **still image**.

## Morph between photos

Turn one photo into another for real: eyes, nose and mouth shift and warp to match (it is not a crossfade).

1. Tap **Morph** in the bottom bar and pick **2 or more photos**. It works best with front-facing faces.
2. Choose **Slow zoom**, **Back and forth** or both, then tap **Create**. The first time it downloads the face model (about 4 MB).
3. Vixa detects the face mesh (468 points) in each photo, straightens and frames it by the eyes, and builds the morph. It lands on the timeline as a normal image clip: transform, effects, filters, keyframes, text, export…

**Chains:** with 3 or more photos the morph goes from one to the next, holding on each.

**No recognisable face** (objects, animals, side views): the editor opens so you can place the points yourself.

**Edit morph** (with the clip selected):
- **Points:** a tap adds a point on every photo; drag it on each photo to the same feature. Long-press to delete it. With faces you can also add your own points (hair, ears…); the face points are small and fixed.
- **Framing:** pinch, rotate and drag to crop each photo.
- **Hold on each photo** and **Back and forth**.

Everything is local: photos never leave your phone.

# {icon:ContentCut} 5. Clip tools

Tap a clip and the toolbar changes. These are all of them, with what each one does. Some only appear for certain clip types.

## {icon:ContentCut} Split, {icon:Delete} Delete, {icon:ContentCopy} Duplicate

- **Split** cuts the clip at the playhead. It asks whether to **split**, **keep the previous part** or **keep the following part**.
- **Delete** removes the clip (on the main track, the others close the gap).
- **Duplicate** makes a copy right after it.

## {icon:Speed} Speed

- **Speed** control (0.1× to 10×) and **Keep pitch** so voices do not sound like a chipmunk or a monster.
- **Speed curves:** presets (ramps, slow motion, acceleration…) or **Custom**: points over the clip where you decide the speed of each section. Points can also be animated with keyframes.

## {icon:VolumeUp} Volume

- Clip **Volume**, **Fade in** and **Fade out**, **Mute** and **Detach audio** (extracts the sound of the video to its own audio track).

## {icon:GraphicEq} Audio effects

- 3-band **equalizer**: **Bass**, **Mids**, **Treble**.
- **Pitch and voice effects:** change the pitch and apply voices (robot, echo, chipmunk…).
- **Cleanup:** **Reduce noise** and **Normalize volume**.
- **Automatic ducking:** mark a clip as **voice** and another as **music that lowers when there is a voice**; the lowered volume is set with a slider.
- You also get shortcuts to AI functions here: *Reduce noise*, *Separate voice and music* and *Text to speech*.

## {icon:Transform} Transform

- **Scale**, **Rotation**, **Position X / Y** and **Opacity**.
- **Fit:** **Fit** (enters whole), **Fill** (fills the canvas by cropping) or **Stretch**.
- **Blend mode:** *Normal*, *Screen*, *Add*, *Multiply*, *Lighten*, *Darken* (to mix layers with light effects).
- **Flip H / V** and **Reset**.

## {icon:Animation} Animate

Three moments: **In**, **Out** and **Loop**. You pick an animation and its **duration**. *Loop* holds the slow camera moves (**Ken Burns**: zooms and pans) and effects like *heartbeat* or *shake*.

## {icon:FilterVintage} Filters

- More than 60 filters grouped by style (vivid, cinema, vintage, black and white, nature…) with an **Intensity** slider.
- **LUT (.cube):** import your own professional color tables.
- If you add an **adjustment layer** ({icon:Layers}), the filter applies to everything below it.

## {icon:Tune} Color adjustments

**Exposure, Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Temperature, Tint, Highlights, Shadows, Hue, Lightness, Fade, Sharpness, Vignette, Grain** and **Curves** (master and per channel: blacks, shadows, midtones, highlights, whites). **Reset all** sets everything to zero. Double-tapping a slider resets it.

## {icon:AutoAwesome} Effects

Library of real-time effects: glitch, VHS, blurs, waves, mirrors, kaleidoscope, rain, snow, flares and more. Tap **Add effect**, tweak its parameters and stack as many as you want (you can **remove** each one). Favorites are marked with a long press.

## {icon:SwapHoriz} Transition

Only on the main track. Choose among **more than 35** (fades, slides, pushes, wipes, circles, zoom, spin, glitch, pixels, waves…) and its **duration**. **Apply to all** puts it between all clips. The first clip has no transition (it has no previous one).

## {icon:Crop} Crop

Remove edges with **Left, Right, Top, Bottom**, or use **Crop to ratio** (1:1, 16:9…). **Reset** restores the original framing.

## {icon:Palette} Chroma key

Removes a background color (**Green** or **Blue** or any you choose) so you can put another video behind. Adjust **Similarity** and **Smoothness**, and **Remove spill** to clean the green tint at the edges.

## {icon:BlurOn} Blur zone

An area of **blur** or **mosaic** (to hide faces or license plates). Drag on the preview to move it and pinch to resize; it can be **elliptical**. With the **diamonds** (keyframes) you can make it follow an object.

## {icon:Texture} Mask

Cuts the clip with a shape: **Linear**, **Circular** or **Rectangular**. Adjust position, **Width**, **Height**, **Feather**, **Rotation** and **Invert**. Drag on the preview to move it and pinch to resize.

## {icon:Wallpaper} Canvas (clip background)

Opens the **Canvas** panel to choose what shows behind the video when it does not fill the whole frame: color, gradient or **blurred video**.

## {icon:Gradient} Fade to black

Toggles a short **start and end** fade on the clip (applied or removed with a tap).

## {icon:SwapVert} To overlay / To main track

Moves the clip between the main track and an overlay track (picture in picture) and back.

## {icon:SwapHoriz} Replace

Swaps the clip's media for another one **keeping** all its settings (duration, filters, effects).

## {icon:AcUnit} Freeze frame

Turns the playhead instant into a **still image** inserted in the clip (great for dramatic pauses).

## {icon:FastRewind} Reverse

Plays the clip **backwards**. A file is prepared in the background.

## {icon:Vibration} Stabilize

Reduces the shake of handheld footage. Motion is analyzed and corrected; toggled on and off with a tap.

## {icon:MyLocation} Track object

Sticks text, emojis, stickers, images, GIFs or video to **something that moves**. It works in two phases: **analyze** (you tap the object, adjust the box and its position is tracked) and **insert** (you choose what to insert, its **original** or **relative** position, and it is placed with keyframes). You can insert several elements with a single analysis. If the object disappears for a moment, the element is **hidden** (can be turned off). A reusable analysis appears in a list.

> **Tip:** for tracking to work better, mark something with **detail** (an eye, a logo, a corner), not a plain area such as the sky.

## {icon:Diamond} Keyframes

They animate any property over time. Move the playhead and tap the **diamond** next to a control to **add or remove** a keyframe; sliders with a diamond can be animated. There are easing curves: *Linear, Ease in, Ease out, Smooth, Overshoot, Bounce, Elastic, Hold*. You can jump to the previous or next keyframe.

# {icon:MusicNote} 7. Audio, music and voice-over

## Music and sounds

With {icon:MusicNote} **Audio** you add a song from your device or from the **Online** tab (Jamendo for music, Freesound for effects). Remember you can pick a **section** of the song without cutting it.

## {icon:Mic} Voice-over

Record your voice **while the video plays**: playback starts when you begin and the voice is placed at the playhead. It records in high quality (mono, 48 kHz).

> **Tip:** use **headphones**. Without them, the microphone also records the speaker sound and the voice loses quality.

## Automatic ducking

To make the music lower by itself when you speak: mark the voice clip as **voice** and turn on **Lower the music when there is a voice** (or **Lower this clip when there is a voice** on the music). If the clip has voice detected with AI, the music lowers **only in the sections where someone speaks**. The **lowered volume** is adjustable.

## Beat cuts and markers

**Extras** has tools that use the rhythm of the music (see *Extras*).

# {icon:AutoAwesome} 8. AI features

All of them run **on your phone**: nothing is uploaded to the Internet. **Models** are downloaded the first time you use each feature (with a progress bar, resumable and verified) and can be managed or deleted from **Manage models**.

Open the panel with {icon:AutoAwesome} **AI** in the toolbar. It is split into three groups.

## Subtitles and voice

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|

# {icon:IosShare} 12. Export

Tap **Export** (top right). You can leave the app or turn off the screen: the export **continues in the background** with a progress notification.

## What you can export

| Type | Result |
|---|---|
| **Video** | MP4 (or WebM on compatible devices) with the codec you choose. |
| **Audio (M4A)** | Only the sound. |
| **Animated GIF** | With compression, boomerang and lots of control. |
| **Frame (PNG / JPG)** | One image of the instant you choose. |

## Video settings

- **Presets:** save your combinations and reuse them.
- **Resolution** (480p to 4K) and **Frames per second.**
- **Codec:** H.264 (most compatible), H.265/HEVC (smaller) and AV1 if your device supports it.
- **Quality:** **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Custom** with **bitrate**.
- **Copy without re-encoding when possible:** if the project is just a sequence of untouched videos, it is copied instantly and without quality loss.
- **Hardware acceleration:** on by default; turn it off if it causes problems.
- **Also save the cover as JPG.**

## Only a section

Turn on **Export only a section**, set **Start** and **End** (or use **Start/End at playhead**) and watch the section in a preview **loop**.

## GIF options

- **Width**, **Frames per second** and **Repeat in a loop.**
- **Boomerang (back and forth):** plays the section forward and backward so the end links with the start (the file weighs about double).
- **Compression:** **None**, **Light**, **Medium**, **Strong** (skips areas that do not change and reduces colors; *Light* is a good balance).
- **Speed** and **Dithering** (smoother gradients, bigger file).

## Destination and name

By default it is saved in `Vixa/Videos`, `Vixa/Audio`, `Vixa/GIF` or `Vixa/Imágenes` depending on the type. You can **Change** the folder and the **file name**. At the top you will see the **estimated size** and the **free space**. When done: **Open**, **Share** or **Export again**.

# {icon:Warning} 17. FAQ and troubleshooting

## Export fails or is very slow
- Try **turning off hardware acceleration** in the export dialog.
- Lower the **resolution** or the **bitrate**; check the **free space**.
- If the app closes while exporting, check the **Error log** (Settings → Help).

## The preview stutters
- Lower the **Preview quality** in Settings or turn on **proxies** for heavy media.
- In the assistant, the preview is rendered first so it plays smoothly.

## Media is missing when opening a project
Open {icon:Save} **Project → Missing media** and **Relink** each one.

## An AI model does not download
Check your connection. The app retries with an alternative address and resumes where it left off. You can see and delete models in **AI → Manage models**.

## I cannot save to the Vixa folder
The app needs the **all files access** permission. Grant it in the system settings and reopen the app.

## Where are my projects stored?
Projects being edited live inside the app; **exported** ones (`.vixa`) and everything you generate go to `Vixa/…`. Make a copy with **Export project (.vixa) → With media** to keep them outside.

## Does it work without Internet?
Yes, almost everything. Only **online resources**, the assistant's **automatic music** and the **download of AI models** (once) need a connection.

# {icon:Menu} 18. Glossary

- **Clip:** each piece (video, photo, audio, text, sticker…) on the timeline.
- **Playhead:** the vertical line that marks the current instant.
- **Keyframe:** a value saved at an instant; between two keyframes the property is animated.
- **Ken Burns:** a slow zoom or pan over a photo.
- **LUT:** a color table that gives a "look" (`.cube` files).
- **Chroma key:** removing a background color (green screen).
- **Ducking:** automatically lowering the music when there is a voice.
- **BPM:** beats per minute, the tempo of a song.
- **Proxy:** a light copy of a heavy media file so editing stays smooth.
- **Boomerang:** playing forward and then backward.
- **.vixa:** Vixa's project format, which can be shared with or without media.
